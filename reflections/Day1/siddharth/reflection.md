# Day 1 reflections

**When I first saw the problem statement, I thought the real task was...**
making a tool that helps teenagers learn things in a more active manner rather than how they would learn by interacting with modern-day llms which simply reveal the answers to questions.

**The first thing I personally wanted to do was...**
think more about the idea since the problem statement is pretty broad and allows for many varying solutions to the problem. As a result, I thought discussion was key for the team to agree to narrow the scope of the problem and focus on it in a particular direction.

**The first thing our team actually did was...**
discuss a few ideas on the problem statement. We had varying approaches, some of which seemed to flow together but some which are kind of mutually exclusive of each other.

**That helped because...**
it broadened everyone's perspective on the problem and introduced new angles for us to think of solutions.

**That hurt because...**
so many diverging ideas made it difficult to settle on one direction to pursue for our project.

**One assumption I made about teenagers, learning or AI was...**
that having continuous feedback during the learning process is actually beneficial, but that depends on the type of feedback as well as the personality of the student, which are important aspects that we need to take into account.
