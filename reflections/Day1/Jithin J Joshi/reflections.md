When I first saw the problem statement, I thought the real task was... to build a tool that would act like a learning tutor ai to guide the user to ask better prompts and asking feedback on how they think and accordingly tutor them to use ai effectively.

The first thing I personally wanted to do was... Make an ai that when given a prompt would ask the user to specify and breakdown problem into subparts and tell what they know and what they don't. Accordingly the ai will nudge the user to make good prompts with clear goals. 

The first thing our team actually did was... Discuss the ambiguity and open-endedness in the problem statement to converge upon and agree on what the problem wants from us. 

That helped because... It clarified the loopholes in an individual's perspective and led to an overall better interpretation of the statement. 

That hurt because... Due to the open ended nature of the problem, it would have been better to spend less time on agreeing upon what the problem statement meant and instead to agree on a baseline and modify it as it goes. 

One assumption I made about teenagers, learning or AI was... The user intends to learn how to make good use of it instead of wanting to have direct answers.
