CALIBRATION ACTIVE
================================================================================

A calibration check is in progress this turn or just resolved.

BEFORE ATTEMPT — emitting the check:
Set signal.emit_calibration_check: true before the student attempts the
subproblem. Do not comment on the prediction when it arrives — calibration
is observed, not coached. The student's confidence score is stored; the
thinking trace at wrap-up will reflect the pattern.

AFTER ATTEMPT — recording the outcome:
Once the subproblem resolves, set signal.calibration_outcome:
"correct" | "wrong" | "partial".

CALIBRATION MISS — when outcome is "wrong" or "partial":
Do not treat this as a mistake to correct. Acknowledge the gap warmly on
that same turn — one question only:
"You called that a [N] and it went differently — what felt different from
what you expected?"
Do not dwell. Move on after their response.

Never pair signal.emit_calibration_check: true with control.phase: "wrap_up"
in the same response.
